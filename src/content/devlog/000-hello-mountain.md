HELLO!!!!!!!!!! Welcome to the very first Critter devlog! If you're reading this, you're one of the real ones - the paint is still wet, the mountain is still.. under construction 🚧 and there's game (yet). Grab a rope and let's talk about what I'm building.

## 🧗 What is Critter?

Firstly, Critter is just a codename/working title, it's a **social-focused multiplayer climbing game** - and hopefully *friendslop without the slop*. You and your friends need to reconnect a shattered world after [insert plot device here] breaks it. The whole thing is built around a simple objective: **fuck around with your friends on voice chat and have a  good time.** Everything else is in service of that.

I've always enjoyed climbing games and after recently playing **PEAK** it inspired me to build something with that same chaotic co-op core loop, but with **deeper systems underneath** and the legs to grow over time. A few things I value for the game:


- 😆 **Multiplayer Chaos** - The game is basically a vehicle to help people socialize with each other, You don't just play with your friends, but other teams are on the server and you can troll them too. 
- 🛋️ **...or just chill** — PvE zones for the carebears who only want to vibe, climb, and not get shoved off a cliff by a stranger. There are co-op puzzles/secrets which you can solve together for cool unlocks.
- 🏔️ **A real progression loop** - As you play the game more, you will get new abilities/items which gives a sense of progression, But a skilled player can always outplay others
- 🔁 **Built for growth** - I'm using my experience to build a game which can grow after launch with new activities and areas. And using procedural generation so you can roll a new world and play through the main missions again if you want. 

## 📓 What to expect from these devlogs

I want this to be a useful, honest record of building a game - not marketing BS. Here's the what to expect:

- 📊 **Monthly progress updates** - the dry, honest stuff: what actually got built this month, and any interesting things I learned along the way.
- ✨ **Feature spotlights** -  showing off a cool new mechanic and genuinely asking: *where should this go next?* Give me feedback.

Plus some misc posts if I find anything worth sharing. 

## 🤖 A note on how it's made (yes, with AI)

Quick bit of transparency, because I'd rather you hear it from me: I've got **10+ years as a technical artist** and have shipped games solo before - but devving a game *this* size and getting it done realistically means leaning on some help.

So I use **AI as a force multiplier.** Most of the work is done myself, but I do use AI to help me understand/write code and summarize changelists to generate progress reports.  I do not intend to use any AI-Generated assets such as Textures, Sounds, Models etc in the project.
